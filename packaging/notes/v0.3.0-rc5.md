## AstraBridge 0.3.0 release candidate 5

### Movement outcomes and diagnostics

- Fixed horizontal movement being reported as successful when the player only moved vertically. Jumping up and falling back down against an obstacle now reports `no_horizontal_progress` for the failed horizontal input.
- Added outcome summaries that distinguish time limits from previously encountered blockers, retain stalled-attempt counts and report actual horizontal endpoint displacement.
- `revisit` preserves earlier route-attempt diagnostics even when a later attempt exhausts the time budget. The route planner and retry policy remain unchanged.
- `sequence` retains each step's feedback and stops dependent steps on unsuccessful movement or failed expectations. Its summary identifies the stopping step and preserves diagnostics before compact output trims older steps.
- Added the optional `expect.min_horizontal_displacement_m` condition. Missing or non-comparable movement measurements fail the condition instead of being treated as zero.
- Added `action-result REQUEST_ID --section summary` to retrieve a stored summary without advancing the game or capturing another image.
- Updated the gameplay skill with result interpretation and a response-wrapper example that preserves feedback, movement details, screenshots and errors.

### Downloads and update

- **Linux:** download the x86_64 AppImage and make it executable.
- **Windows:** extract the x86_64 portable ZIP and run `astrabridge.exe`. Keep the extracted files together.
- Existing container installations: open the new application and explicitly update the runtime in Setup. Managed profiles, saves, Atlas and knowledge are preserved.
- **Export the gameplay skill again after updating**, and give the new export to your agent.
- The matching runtime is `ghcr.io/incident201/astrabridge-runtime:v0.3.0-rc5`; its exact OCI digest is embedded in the application and included in `release.json`.

Supply your own Morrowind installation. See the [system requirements](https://github.com/incident201/AstraBridgeForOpenMW/blob/v0.3.0-rc5/docs/system-requirements.md) and [installation instructions](https://github.com/incident201/AstraBridgeForOpenMW/blob/v0.3.0-rc5/README.md).
