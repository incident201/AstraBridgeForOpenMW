================================================================
              Yet Another Idle Animation Fix v1.1
                        Author: Qlonever
================================================================

This is an animation replacement mod that fixes idle drifting, without visually altering the animations.

----------------------------------------------------------------
                          DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------------

There are plenty of existing mods that fix the idle animation drifting problem in Morrowind. (If you don't know what that is, it's what causes strider NPCs to fall off of their platforms.) However, I haven't seen one that doesn't mess up the animations in some way, like making NPCs' feet slide across the ground.

Instead of just deleting the root node motion that causes the NPCs to move, this mod completely transfers the motion to the pelvis bone. This preserves the visual movement while preventing the animations from changing the characters' actual coordinates. In addition to the basic standing animations, this also covers idle animations for swimming, sneaking, casting, holding weapons, and being knocked out from fatigue loss.
